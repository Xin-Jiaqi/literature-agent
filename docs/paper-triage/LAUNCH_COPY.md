## Public post

I extracted a small part of my research workflow into a free, open-source agent skill: **Paper Triage**.

Give your agent one paper and your research goal. It guides a reading decision around the contribution, evidence locations, limitations, relevance and one concrete next step.

The part I care about most: making it clear what the agent actually read, which claims come from the paper, and what still needs checking.

Two physics/materials examples, the instructions and the validation limits are here:
https://github.com/Xin-Jiaqi/literature-agent/tree/main/docs/paper-triage

If you try it, I'd like to hear whether it helps you decide what to read next—and where the evidence mapping goes wrong.
